# Product

## Register

product

## Users
FRILO serves entrepreneurs, independent professionals, small businesses, and early teams in Benin and West Africa who need a credible web presence without managing a custom agency project from scratch. The primary client is practical and time-constrained: they want to choose a template, understand the price, pay safely, submit useful project details, and track delivery. Super-admin users operate catalogue, orders, payments, settings, contact requests, reviews, notifications, backups, and audits from the Laravel backoffice.

## Product Purpose
FRILO turns website creation into a guided commerce and delivery workflow: discover sector-specific templates, order with clear pricing, pay through a trusted local-compatible flow, and follow delivery from the dashboard. Success means users trust the offer before paying, understand what FRILO needs from them, and never wonder what the next step is.

## Brand Personality
Reliable, premium, accessible. FRILO should sound expert and reassuring, with commercial confidence but no pressure. The experience should feel locally aware through payment clarity, language, support expectations, and practical workflows, not through decorative cultural shortcuts.

## Anti-references
Do not make FRILO feel like a generic imported SaaS template with vague claims, purple-blue gradients everywhere, and artificial dashboards. Avoid heavy administrative styling, decorative African cliches, forced pan-African color symbolism, jargon-heavy copy, inaccessible luxury signals, and long funnels that hide price, payment, or delivery state. Avoid interfaces that feel like a public institution, an agency portfolio with no operational depth, or a checkout that asks for trust without showing evidence.

## Design Principles
- Make trust operational: show price, payment state, order state, and next action clearly.
- Keep premium practical: polished enough to reassure, simple enough for small businesses to act.
- Respect local context without decoration: clarity, language, payment, and support matter more than motifs.
- Reduce cognitive load in the order tunnel: one decision at a time, visible progress, recoverable errors.
- Preserve role clarity: public, client, and admin surfaces should never feel interchangeable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG AA for contrast, focus visibility, labels, keyboard navigation, and status communication. Respect reduced motion, avoid color-only order/payment states, keep French copy concise on mobile, and design for users who may be comparing templates or paying from a phone under imperfect network conditions.
