# Product

## Register

product

## Users

FRILO serves three user groups. Visitors are TPE/PME owners, independents, and local service providers evaluating website templates and pricing before ordering. Clients are authenticated buyers who need to submit project details, pay, and track delivery without technical complexity. Admin users are the FRILO operations team responsible for catalog content, payments, production follow-up, client communication, and delivery quality.

## Product Purpose

FRILO lets small businesses order a professional personalized website in minutes, with human delivery in 48 hours. Success means a visitor can understand the offer quickly, select a template, complete the order/payment flow, and trust the delivery process through transparent client and admin status tracking.

## Brand Personality

Clear, dependable, and energetic. FRILO should feel like a practical local business partner: direct about price and deadlines, polished enough to inspire confidence, and simple enough for non-technical users to complete the journey without hesitation.

## Anti-references

FRILO should not look like a vague AI website builder, a generic SaaS dashboard, a dark developer tool, or a decorative agency portfolio that hides pricing and delivery reality. Avoid implying that AI generates client websites; the positioning is explicitly human production, zero hidden fees, and support availability.

## Design Principles

1. Make the business promise inspectable: price, timeline, status, and next action should be visible where decisions happen.
2. Separate roles clearly: public discovery, client tracking, and admin operations must feel connected but never mixed.
3. Prefer operational clarity over spectacle: the interface should reduce uncertainty and move the user to the next concrete step.
4. Preserve trust with data truthfulness: order, payment, template, and delivery states must mirror backend reality.
5. Use brand energy sparingly: blue/purple accents should guide attention, not dominate every surface.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for public, client, and admin surfaces. All interactive controls need keyboard focus states, form errors must be connected to fields, motion must respect reduced-motion preferences, and template/preview imagery needs descriptive alt text.
